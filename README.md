### 🌟 What is VerbaClear?
VerbaClear bridges the gap between complex information and human understanding.

- 🧠 **Dynamic Complexity Slider:** Instantly adjust reading levels from original text down to ELI5 analogies.
- 👁️ **Visual Accessibility Suite:** Bionic Reading toggles, OpenDyslexic font, focus line trackers, and low-strain contrast presets.
- 🔊 **Voice & Conversational AI:** Expressive text-to-speech engine with an interactive "Ask the Document" audio assistant.
- 🔍 **Inline Jargon Decoder:** Click or hover over complex legal, medical, or technical terms for instant, plain-English definitions.
